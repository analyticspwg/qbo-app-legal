Privacy Policy

PWI Claude Writer · Operated by Pearl West Investments Ltd. · Effective October 13, 2026

1. Scope

PWI Claude Writer (the "App") is a private, internal tool used only by Pearl West Investments Ltd. and its affiliated companies (together, "we" or "us") to read and update our own QuickBooks Online accounting records. The App is not offered to the public or to any outside business. This policy explains what data the App handles and how.

2. Data the App accesses

When an authorized administrator connects one of our QuickBooks Online companies, the App can access that company's accounting data through Intuit's API, including the chart of accounts, journal entries, transactions, vendors, customers, classes and company profile. The App requests only the QuickBooks Online Accounting scope. It does not request payment processing access and cannot charge cards or move funds.

3. How the data is used

Data is used only to perform internal bookkeeping and reporting tasks requested by our own staff, such as preparing and posting journal entries, correcting classifications and reconciling records. Every change is reviewed and approved by a staff member before it is posted.

4. Service providers

The App runs on equipment we control. To carry out requested tasks, accounting data may be processed by the following service providers acting on our behalf:

  • Intuit Inc., which hosts the QuickBooks Online data.
  • Anthropic PBC, whose AI assistant (Claude) helps staff prepare and review accounting changes.

We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Storage and security

OAuth access credentials are stored only on our own computers in files restricted to the authorized operator account. They are never embedded in source code or shared. A local log records each change the App makes, including the record's state before and after the change, for audit and reversal purposes. Access tokens expire after one hour and are refreshed automatically.

6. Retention

Credentials are kept only while a company remains connected. Audit logs are kept for as long as needed for our accounting and record-keeping obligations. The underlying accounting data remains in QuickBooks Online under Intuit's own policies.

7. Disconnecting

An administrator of any connected company can revoke the App's access at any time from QuickBooks Online under Settings → Apps (or Intuit's connected apps page). Revoking access immediately stops the App from reading or writing data for that company, and we will delete the related stored credentials.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the App changes. The effective date above shows when it was last revised.
